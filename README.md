# Ilayaraja Portfolio Mobile App

An AI-powered Android portfolio application showcasing **Ilayaraja’s professional journey, leadership experience, technical expertise, projects, and achievements** in Digital & Cloud Transformation and AI-Led Automation.

## 🚀 Overview

The Ilayaraja Portfolio Mobile App provides a centralized platform to explore professional experience, key accomplishments, technical skills, certifications, and portfolio projects. It reflects a commitment to engineering excellence, AI-driven innovation, and continuous learning.

## ✨ Features

- **Professional Profile** — Career summary, leadership experience, and areas of expertise.
- **Experience & Achievements** — Highlights of digital transformation and Quality Engineering initiatives.
- **Portfolio Projects** — Showcases of AI-powered solutions, automation accelerators, and technology projects.
- **Technical Skills** — Cloud, Quality Engineering, AI-Led Automation, Generative AI, AWS, and Azure.
- **Certifications & Education** — Professional certifications and academic qualifications.
- **AI-Powered Experience** — Interactive AI capabilities to enhance portfolio exploration.
- **Contact & Professional Links** — Easy access to professional profiles and contact information.
